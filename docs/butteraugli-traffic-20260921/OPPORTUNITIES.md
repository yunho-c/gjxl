# Butteraugli filtering and reduction traffic investigation

Goal: investigate large, high-effort workloads until the source, experiments, and remaining-hypothesis audit support an empirical stopping conclusion. **Complete at the user-approved practical stopping point.** The final [report](REPORT.md) and [hypothesis audit](HYPOTHESIS-AUDIT.md) give the conclusion; the inventory below also retains the exploratory measurements that led to it. A failed tuning sweep alone does not establish exhaustion.

Baseline: isolated detached `4f3e414` checkout, containing current main `b1a7373` plus the production-aligned profiling changes used by the September 21 stage study. Kernel source is identical to main. Existing numerical tolerances remain fixed. This first phase preserves arithmetic and output; any later numerical changes need independent decoded quality/rate qualification.

## Evidence gates

1. Freeze baseline/candidate sources, binaries, shader libraries, inputs, commands, and logs. Preserve an append-only experiment ledger and resumable case records.
2. Compare guarded kernels with odd/edge/tiny sizes, multiple data patterns, nontrivial strides, all output planes, unchanged inputs/padding, and existing error handling.
3. Screen mechanisms on large grids; paired GPU microtiming is diagnostic. It does not qualify an encoder improvement.
4. Integrate promising candidates individually. Measure production-aligned stage timing separately from ordinary complete-call timing. Include baseline-versus-baseline controls and fixed independent confirmation cohorts.
5. Large-image scope must cover at least 12/24/48 MP, multiple photograph contents, e7/e10 and more than one target, plus smaller/odd-size regression controls. Avoid a general claim based on one image, transform mix, or target.
6. Preserve existing metric/AQ contracts, codestream determinism, decoding, dimensions and finite pixels. Any changed numerical/output behavior needs decoded baseline/candidate quality/rate evidence; do not widen tolerances.
7. Audit every inventory item and every new mechanism suggested by results. Mark rejected, retained, or unresolved with authoritative evidence. Unresolved substantial plausible opportunities prevent a practical exhaustion claim. Small, uncertain or workload-specific effects are disclosed rather than treated as proof of a hardware optimum; the user explicitly selected this practical stopping rule.

The directly confirmed original-baseline speedup is 12.697%/10.715% for the two large flagship settings. All planned campaigns are complete. The exact-output Butteraugli study has reached its practical stopping point for this hardware and covered workloads; it cannot prove optimality over all future algorithms. The final prototype is preserved in the isolated worktree, with its smaller incremental results reported separately rather than combined across cohorts.
